01 — About

A short introduction

I started out writing PHP form handlers in a small agency in Chennai, and never really left the web. Since then I've worked across the stack, but my attention keeps returning to the front end — the part people touch, where a 200 millisecond delay is the difference between a good product and a frustrating one.

Most of my work sits in design systems and product front-ends: turning a set of Figma frames into a component library that a dozen engineers can build from without stepping on each other. I care about accessibility as a baseline rather than a pass at the end, and about performance budgets that get defended in code review.

Outside of work I maintain two small open-source packages, read far more than I write, and make a slow filter coffee that my flatmate refuses to drink.
